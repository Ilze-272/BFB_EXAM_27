Welcome to FoodShare Pretoria

FoodShare is a community-driven platform connecting restaurants, shops, charities, and individuals to reduce food waste in Pretoria. We believe that no good food should go to waste when there are people who could benefit from it.

For Businesses

Restaurants and shops can list surplus food instead of throwing it away, helping the community while reducing waste.

For Charities

Charities can find and reserve food donations to support those in need, ensuring resources reach the right people.

For Individuals

Community members can discover affordable or free food items, helping families save money and reduce household waste.

The Food Waste Challenge

In South Africa, approximately 10 million tonnes of food is wasted every year. This happens at every stage—from production to consumption—while millions of people face food insecurity.

In Pretoria alone, restaurants, shops, and households dispose of edible food daily. Meanwhile, many individuals and families struggle to access nutritious meals.

FoodShare bridges this gap by creating a simple, efficient way to connect surplus food with those who need it most, right here in our community.

How It Works

Three simple steps to start making a difference

1

List Your Food

Restaurants, shops, or individuals create a listing with details about surplus food, including quantity, expiry date, and pickup location.

2

Find & Reserve

Browse available listings in your area. Reserve items you need—whether free or at a reduced price—and get pickup details instantly.

3

Collect & Enjoy

Pick up your reserved food during the specified time window. Good food finds a new home, and waste is prevented!
